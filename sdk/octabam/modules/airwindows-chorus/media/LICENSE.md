# Actual Octatrack LCD exports

Captured from the private Air Chorus 0.1.0-experimental native image with
headless `ot_emu`, MKII panel. Original framebuffer pixels were preserved at
integer scale 6 in two grayscale colors. Capture plan, emulator/image hashes
and PNG hashes are recorded in `capture.json`. Captures were visually reviewed.
They document menu location, confirmed custom-effect controls, example and dry.
